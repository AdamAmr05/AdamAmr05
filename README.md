# Hey, I’m Adam.

I build software, from the idea and backend architecture to how it works and feels. I care a little too much sometimes.

My work spans product design, interfaces, interactions, backend systems, and AI systems. I’m building several things, from freight software and customer support tools to accommodation research, collaborative learning, and basketball games.

### Selected work

**[Marevia](https://adamamr.me/projects/marevia/)**  
Freight software for providers and their customers, connecting requests, quotes, shipments, tracking, and documents. Includes an AI assistant that works across live operational data and helps prepare actions for review.

**[RelayChat / Ash AI](https://adamamr.me/projects/relaychat/)**  
An embeddable AI shopping and support assistant with image-based product search, voice, customizable interfaces, and streamed conversations that recover across refreshes and devices.

**[Prism](https://prismcanvas.app/)**  
A collaborative AI workspace that turns documents and questions into interactive maps of ideas, with real-time collaboration, source-grounded diagrams, learning tools, and follow-up conversations.

**[Found](https://adamamr.me/projects/found/)**  
An accommodation research app with interactive 3D maps and email editing built into the conversation. Connects finding somewhere to stay with contacting it and following the reply.

[Source code](https://github.com/AdamAmr05/found)

**[Better 82-0](https://adamamr.me/projects/better-82-0/)**  
A basketball game I rebuilt around a more nuanced scoring model. Reached more than 800,000 visits in about a week.

**[CampusCare](https://adamamr.me/projects/campuscare/)**  
A mobile app for reporting university maintenance issues, assigning the work, and following it through resolution and approval.
